About

Home Project Calculators is a set of free tools that work out how much material a home or yard project needs: cabinets, countertops, asphalt, stain, propane, resin, epoxy, sod and sand. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Each page explains its formula and the standard sizes it assumes, and every assumption can be changed to match your supplier.
