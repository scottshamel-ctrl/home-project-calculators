Free Home Project Calculators

Work out how much material to buy before you order: cabinets, countertops, asphalt, stain, propane, resin, epoxy, sod and sand. No signup, and it works on your phone.

  • Kitchen Cabinet CalculatorEnter your wall lengths and appliance widths. You get the linear feet, a set of standard cabinet widths that fills the space, and a cost estimate.
  • Countertop CalculatorEnter each countertop section, your island and backsplash height. You get the square footage, how many slabs to buy and the estimated cost.
  • Asphalt CalculatorEnter the area and compacted thickness. You get the tons of asphalt to order and the cost.
  • Deck and Fence Stain CalculatorEnter your deck, railing and fence sizes. You get the surface area and how many gallons of stain to buy.
  • Propane Usage CalculatorEnter your appliance's BTU rating, how long it runs and your tank size. You get how long the tank lasts and what it costs per month.
  • Epoxy Resin CalculatorEnter the size and depth of your pour. You get the mixed volume and how much resin and hardener to measure.
  • Epoxy Floor CalculatorEnter your floor size and coating thickness. You get the coverage per gallon, total gallons and how many kits to buy.
  • Sod CalculatorEnter your lawn's length and width. You get the square feet, pallets and pieces to order and the cost.
  • Sand CalculatorEnter the area and depth. You get the volume, the weight in tons and how many bags to buy.
